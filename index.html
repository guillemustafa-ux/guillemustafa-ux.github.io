09

Questions traders ask first

Short answers. The long ones are in the scripts and repos above.

Does your code repaint?
The strategies and alerts I write are built not to, and you can check it rather than take my word for it. Where a public script includes a repainting or look-ahead form, it is labeled and shown next to the honest one: the Repaint Auditor plots three request.security forms side by side and counts intrabar flicker and ghost signals live, and the ICT strategy has a look-ahead demo switch that is off by default.
How do you model fills and costs?
Commission and slippage come from your broker's numbers, not defaults. Cost Ladder re-prices the same trades at 0x, 1x and 2x costs so you see the break-even. When a stop and a target fit in the same bar, Intrabar Ambiguity settles it with lower-timeframe data instead of an assumed path.
Who owns the code?
You do. Source is delivered with each milestone. Nothing derived from your rules or data is published, reused or shown to anyone else; the public work on this page is my own.
Do you sign an NDA?
Yes. Send yours and I sign it once I have read it, or I can use a standard mutual NDA, before you share the rules. Confidentiality is the default anyway, with or without a document.
Do you guarantee profitability?
No. A backtest is a claim about the past. What I deliver is the set of checks that shows how much of that claim survives costs, sample size and real execution, so you decide with the numbers in front of you. Anyone promising returns is selling something else.
Do you do calls?
No. Everything goes through the Upwork chat in writing, which keeps every requirement and decision on record and spares both of us the timezone math. Buenos Aires, UTC-3; I usually reply within the working day.

Tell me your strategy.

I usually reply within the working day. Work starts from a written scope: what gets built, what gets checked, and what "done" means.
